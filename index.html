§ 01 / The gap

The firm corrects its AI. The next model doesn’t know.

A lawyer catches a missed clause, fixes the answer and moves on. That judgment is valuable—but unless it becomes a reusable test, the firm has little way to check whether the same error returns.

§ 02 / The mechanism

From legal work to a private test.

caly is designed to connect the evidence a firm already produces in its AI workflows: the request, the documents, the output and the lawyer’s verified correction. The correction becomes a test the firm can use again.

§ 04 / Scope

First, make the knowledge measurable. Then make it portable.

We’re starting with evaluation. Fine-tuning and self-hosting are longer-term directions, not capabilities available today.

Initial scope · in development

A private evaluation layer

  • Capture tasks, source documents, AI outputs and verified corrections.
  • Turn corrections into firm-specific evaluation cases.
  • Compare models and surface recurring errors and regressions.

Longer term · not yet built

Intelligence the firm can carry forward

  • Use the firm’s verified data to fine-tune open-weight models.
  • Support deployment on the firm’s own infrastructure.
  • Carry evaluations and improvements across AI providers.

Solid = initial scope. Dashed = future direction.
