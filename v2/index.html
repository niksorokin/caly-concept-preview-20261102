Making legal AI learn from the work.

Pre-launch software for law firms. caly is designed to turn lawyers’ verified corrections on AI-assisted work into reusable private tests — run on the firm’s own matters, not public benchmarks.

01 — The gap

Lawyers catch the errors. The fixes vanish.

Every day, lawyers review AI-generated drafts and correct them: a missed clause, a wrong deadline, a misread fact. The correction goes into the document — and nowhere else.

The firm’s judgment is real, repeated and expensive. But once the matter closes, there is no record of what the AI got wrong, how often, or whether the next model version makes the same mistake again.

Public benchmarks don’t answer this. They don’t know the firm’s precedents, its house style, or the clauses its clients actually sign.

02 — The mechanism

A verified correction becomes a private test.

caly is designed to sit inside the firm’s existing legal and AI workflows and keep the evidence each review already produces.

When a lawyer verifies a correction, that moment — the task, the source documents, the AI’s draft and the lawyer’s fix — is intended to become a reusable evaluation case, private to the firm.

  1. 01The task. What the lawyer asked the AI tool to do.
  2. 02The sources. The documents the answer should rest on.
  3. 03The draft. What the model actually produced.
  4. 04The correction. What the lawyer changed, verified as right.
  5. 05The test. A reusable case in the firm’s private evaluation set.

03 — The judgment

Lawyers stay responsible. The errors stop repeating unseen.

caly does not review legal work and does not replace professional judgment. Lawyers verify. The system is designed to remember what verification found.

With a private set of cases grounded in real matters, the firm can test the models it relies on: surface errors that keep recurring across its work, and catch regressions when a provider ships an update that breaks an answer which used to pass.

Nothing here requires lawyers to build datasets or run an ML team. The input is the review they already do.

04 — The horizon

Knowledge the firm owns, across providers.

The initial scope is evaluation: capture, private cases, recurring-error and regression testing. That is what we are building now.

Longer term, the same verified record could travel further — but these are directions, not capabilities available today:

Future direction · not yet built

Fine-tuning open-weight models on the firm’s verified data, portable intelligence that carries across AI providers, and on-prem or self-hosted deployment. None of this is part of the current product scope.
